# SoulPrint Engine Pro

### The AI workforce that knows your business — and ships work that sounds like you wrote it.

SoulPrint Engine Pro is a B2B platform that gives a business owner **a 150+ agent AI workforce** trained on the soul of their company. You drop in a paragraph about who you are and what you stand for, and from that moment every agent — researcher, copywriter, designer, social media manager, salesperson, web automator — produces work that fits your brand voice without you having to keep correcting it.

Think of it as the difference between hiring a freelancer who needs three weeks of onboarding, and hiring one who already read every internal doc, knows your customers, mirrors your tone, and shows up the next morning ready to ship.

## How it works in 30 seconds

1. **Create your Organization.** Tell us your mission, your audience, your tone, your values, and what you stand against. (Or skip it — we'll draft it from your website and you can edit.) This becomes your **Soul** — the brand DNA every agent reads before doing anything.
2. **Build a Team.** Pick from 150+ specialized agents — Brand Strategist, Conversion Copywriter, Social Media Autopilot, Competitive Researcher, Pricing Analyst, Email Sequencer, Frontend Designer, and more. Or just type what you need and the platform routes you to the right one automatically.
3. **Just type.** Ask for a deliverable — *"draft a launch announcement"*, *"design 5 hero images for our landing page"*, *"research the top 3 competitors and tell me what we're missing"*. Agents work in real time, show their reasoning, and ship a finished file.
4. **Ship.** Download as markdown, CSV, image, calendar invite, or have an agent autonomously publish it to your social accounts on a schedule.

## What you can actually do with it

### 1. **Real research, not summaries of summaries**
Every agent has live web search and a real headless browser. Ask *"compare the pricing pages of our top 5 competitors"* and an agent will literally open each site, pull the prices, fill in a comparison table, and write the strategic takeaways. With sources you can click and verify.

### 2. **Mission Mode — autonomous tasks that take more than one step**
Tell an agent *"research the top 10 wedding photographers in Phoenix, find their Instagram handles, and write a one-page outreach plan."* It plans the work, runs it iteratively, hits real websites, fills forms, takes screenshots, and produces a downloadable artifact at the end. You see every step in real time — no black box.

You can:
- Drag-drop your brief, your existing brand guide, your competitor list — agents read them and ground their work in your reality
- Watch missions live with a "Reading the web — competitor.com…" / "Drafting brief…" status feed
- Cancel any time, force-stop a stuck mission, or chain missions together

### 3. **Social Queue — content that runs itself**
A single page where you draft, schedule, and publish to X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Threads, Bluesky, Mastodon, and Reddit.

- Type *"draft 5 LinkedIn posts about our Q1 launch, lean into the engineering-team angle"* — it produces 5 platform-ready drafts in your voice with hashtags, cover images, and your logo composited in the corner.
- Don't know what to post? Click **Suggest topics** and the platform fuses your brand soul with this week's news in your space and proposes 6 specific, postable ideas with citations.
- Click **Auto-post via Mission** on any draft. Pick a date. At that exact moment, an AI agent opens the platform's composer in a real headless browser, pastes your post, and clicks Publish. While you're in a meeting.
- Want to edit the cover image? Hit the pencil. Custom prompt, regenerate, swap the logo to any corner with one click.

### 4. **Brand-aware image and video generation**
Built-in image and video generation. Every render gets your Soul, your visual style guide, and your primary logo composited automatically — so a freshly generated cover image looks like it came out of your brand kit, not out of a stock AI gallery.

- Need 5 cover images for a campaign? Type *"create 5 hero images of our coffee subscription box for the holidays."* You get 5 distinct variations — bold, airy, cinematic, playful, photorealistic — each on-brand and download-ready.
- Need a 30-second product video? Same flow. Same brand fidelity.

### 5. **Group chats with multiple agents at once**
Spin up a group chat with three agents — say, your Brand Strategist, your Copywriter, and your Conversion Analyst — and watch them debate your landing page draft in real time. You're the moderator. They're the team.

### 6. **An organization library that gets smarter the more you use it**
Every artifact an agent ships — every brief, every research doc, every social post, every image — is saved to your **Documents** library. Agents can read what came before. Ask *"build on the brand strategy we wrote last month"* and an agent literally opens the doc and continues the thinking. No re-explaining yourself across sessions.

### 7. **Upload your own files and ground every answer in them**
Drag in PDFs, brand guides, customer interviews, financial data, product specs — agents read them and use them as context. Your data stays inside your Org; we don't train on it; we don't share it.

### 8. **Conversational shortcuts the way you'd actually talk**
- Slash command `/mission` to launch an autonomous run from inside any chat
- Smart auto-routing — type something, the platform picks the right specialist
- Inline image regeneration with custom prompts
- One-click thumbs up/down to teach the platform your preferences
