## Why Foundry Exists

You're not short on AI tools. You're short on **AI workers who actually finish jobs**.

ChatGPT writes drafts. Foundry ships them — researched, branded, image-generated, published to your social channels, audited for quality, and logged against your spend cap. Every agent knows your team's voice, your customers, and your tools, because every team carries a **SoulPrint** (mission, tone, audience, values, north star) that's woven into every prompt automatically.

If you've ever caught yourself paying for ChatGPT *and* Jasper *and* Lindy *and* a VA *and* a junior marketer — Foundry replaces the lot of them with one organisation, one bill, and one place to manage everything.

## 🏢 Multi-Org, Multi-Team, Multi-Brand

- **Multi-org per user** — bring multiple brands or clients under one login. Org limits scale with your plan: **Spark** includes 1 org (with extra-org add-ons at $20/mo each), **Temper** includes 3 orgs, **Refine** is unlimited and multi-workspace.
- **Per-org isolation.** Memory, documents, connections, billing, agents — every byte is scoped to the org. A leak between two of your clients is mechanically impossible.
- **Per-team SoulPrint.** Mission, tone, audience, values, and north star are saved per team and automatically injected into every agent reply, so your luxury jewellery brand never gets the same copy voice as your dental clinic.
- **Roles**: Owner, Admin, Member, Viewer. Invite via email link or shareable URL.
- **Owner deletes, members archive, viewers read** — clean permission ladder, no surprises.

**Benefit:** One operator, ten brands, zero cross-contamination.

**Benefit:** Your AI workers feel like coworkers, not a chat window.

## ✍️ Content & Artifact Generation

- **Markdown-driven Documents** — agents emit `[[ARTIFACT]]` blocks that become real `.pdf` / `.docx` / `.md` / `.html` files in your Document library, downloadable and shareable.
- **`[[APP]]` bundles** — single-prompt working web apps (calculators, quote generators, mini-tools) deployed in-line, sandboxed, and shareable via public URL.
- **`[[IMAGE]]` blocks** — agents auto-generate cover art, ad creative, social graphics via Nano Banana, attached inline to the reply. No "click here to generate" gating — the image ships with the caption.
- **Brand-aware** — every artifact respects the team SoulPrint (palette, tone, voice). Your dental clinic's IG posts and your jewellery brand's IG posts look like they came from different agencies.

**Benefit:** "Draft a 5-slide carousel for Tuesday" → 5 images + caption + hashtags, in one reply.

## 🔍 Research & Web Intelligence

- **Forge Crawl** — our in-house web scraper (no Firecrawl, no AGPL, no SaaS bill). Returns clean markdown from any page, auto-falls-back to headless Chromium for JS-heavy sites, and ships with three mission tools: `web_scrape`, `web_crawl`, `web_extract` (LLM-driven structured extraction).
- **Brave Search integration** — agents have first-class web search inside conversations and missions, with cited sources.
- **Perplexity Sonar** — deep research summaries for audits and competitor reconnaissance.
- **Document RAG** — every PDF / DOCX / text file your team uploads becomes a searchable, agent-readable knowledge source, scoped strictly to the org.

**Benefit:** Your agents know what's actually true on the internet today — not your model's training cutoff.
